Simple Past: Regular and Irregular Verbs

📅1. What is the Simple Past?▶
📄 1.1 Definition and uses▶
I visited my grandmother last Sunday.
She studied English yesterday.

When do we use it?

Finished actions
We finished the exam.
Specific time
I was born in 2005.
Series of actions
He woke up, ate and left.
Past habits
I played baseball every day.

📝2. Regular verbs▶
✏️ 2.1 The -ed rule and spelling▶

Add -ed to the base form. It is the same for every subject (I, you, he, she, it, we, they).

I worked all day.
She cleaned her room.
RuleBasePast
Most verbs: add -edwalkwalked
Ends in -e: add -dloveloved
Consonant + y: change y to -iedstudystudied
Vowel + y: add -edplayplayed
Consonant-vowel-consonant: double the last letterstopstopped

🔀3. Irregular verbs▶
❓ 3.1 What are irregular verbs?▶

They do not take -ed. Their past form changes or stays the same. There is no rule: we need to learn them.

I went to school.
She ate lunch at noon.
We bought new shoes.

Four patterns

Same form
cut – cut, put – put, hit – hit
Vowel change
sing – sang, drink – drank, swim – swam
-ought / -aught
buy – bought, teach – taught
Completely different
go – went, am/is – was

❓4. Negatives and questions▶
🚫 4.1 Negative sentences▶
Affirmative
Subject + past verb
She worked yesterday.
Negative
Subject + didn’t + base verb
She didn’t work yesterday.
Question
Did + subject + base verb?
Did she work yesterday?

The golden rule

After did / didn’t, the verb goes back to the base form.

✗ She didn’t went.

✓ She didn’t go.

💬 4.2 Questions and short answers▶
QuestionAnswer
Did you study?Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.
Did she go to the party?Yes, she did. / No, she didn’t.
Where did you go?I went to the park.
What did she eat?She ate a sandwich.
When did they arrive?They arrived at six.
Why did he leave?He left because he was sick.
